# Solar-system textures

Textures by [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/),
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The images were downloaded without modification and renamed for use in this preview.
The source describes these as NASA-based maps with adjusted colors and some filled gaps;
they are illustrative, not real-time observations.

The overview uses reduced copies of the existing NASA Earth and Viking Mars
textures instead of the Solar System Scope Earth and Mars files below. Their
source and credits are listed in [globe texture credits](../CREDITS.md).

Planet positions are computed from the device clock using [Astronomy Engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy),
a mathematical ephemeris rather than live measurements. Orbital periods and
relative planet sizes are documented by the [NASA Planetary Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/).
The overview compresses distances and exaggerates planet sizes for visibility.

All spherical JPEG maps are 2048 × 1024 pixels.
