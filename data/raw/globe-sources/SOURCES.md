# Original globe image sources

- Earth: NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry, August 2004, 21600 × 10800 global JPEG, https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/ and https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/august/world.topo.bathy.200408.3x21600x10800.jpg. Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
- Mars: Viking Merged Color Mosaic, 64 pixels/degree, 23040 × 11520 global assembled from eight unmodified 5760 × 5760 PNG tiles, https://www.mars.asu.edu/data/mdim_color/ and https://www.mars.asu.edu/data/mdim_color/large/. Credit: NASA Viking Orbiter, USGS Astrogeology Science Center, and Arizona State University Mars Space Flight Facility. Tile order west to east: 225, 315, 045, 135; north then south. The assembled JPEG retains the original dimensions and uses quality 95 and no chroma subsampling.
